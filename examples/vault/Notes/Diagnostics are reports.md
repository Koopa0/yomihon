Everything yomihon finds wrong it reports. It repairs nothing.

A reading surface that rewrote your frontmatter could not be trusted to show you what the file says. The file is yours; you edit it.

## Where they appear

Faults in a note appear beside it, in the right rail. Faults in the vault as a whole — links with no target, notes nothing cites, names two files answer to, statuses outside the list — are gathered on the health page.

[[A note with a fault in its frontmatter]] carries one you can see from here.

## What a diagnostic says

What was found, and what yomihon did about it. A link whose section did not match says both, because a reader told only of the fault cannot tell whether the page in front of them is missing something.
