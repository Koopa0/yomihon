Everything yomihon finds wrong with a note it reports. It never repairs.

That is a product decision rather than a missing feature. A reading surface
that quietly rewrote your frontmatter would be a surface you could not trust to
show you what the file says — and the file is the thing you actually own.

## Where they appear

Beside the note, in the right rail, for faults in that note. On the health page,
gathered, for faults that are about the vault as a whole: links with no target,
notes nothing cites, names two files answer to, statuses outside the schema's
list.

[[A note with a fault in its frontmatter]] carries one you can see from here.

## What a diagnostic says

What was found, and what it did about it. A link whose section did not place
says both, because a reader who is told only the fault cannot tell whether the
page in front of them is missing something.
