Copy this vault's `System/schemas/vault-schema.toml` into your own vault at the same path, then change it until it describes your notes.

The contract is validated as a whole: a half-finished edit switches the status control off until the edit is finished.

Every key it allows has to be one you would really write. Start from the [[Frontmatter]] your notes already carry, and take out what you never use.

Still to write: the order to change things in. Start with [[The status lifecycle]]; it needs the most thought.
